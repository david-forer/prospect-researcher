# Ideal customer

Copy this file to `icp.md` and replace the example with your own.

## Company
- 5 to 20 employees, $1M to $5M revenue
- Founder-led, no dedicated operations leader
- Service businesses: agencies, clinics, trades, professional firms
- Based in the US

## Signals worth finding
- The founder is still in every client conversation
- Hiring an office manager or operations role
- Growth: new locations, new service lines, more staff
- Reviews that mention slow replies, missed follow-ups or scheduling mix-ups

## Who can say yes
Founder, owner, CEO or president. A COO counts. Anyone else is Medium at best.

## Not a fit
- More than 50 staff, or an operations leader already in place
- Franchises and enterprise subsidiaries
